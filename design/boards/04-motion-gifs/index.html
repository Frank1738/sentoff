Motion

Every animation under 400ms, ease-out, no bounce, no parallax. With reduced motion on, cards, numbers and the share image appear instantly.

1 Cards come in

Each manager gets a line in the status first. Their card follows 110ms later: opacity 0 to 1, y 16 to 0, 280ms, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1). The list never scrolls itself.

2 Ratings count up

The whole-number rating and the numbers in the stats line count from 0 in 360ms, cubic ease-out, starting 120ms after the card lands. Tabular numerals; gameweek numbers do not count.

3 The card is shown

One hard snap: opaque on frame one, scale 1.6 to 1 in 110ms, cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1). Upright, no overshoot. Fires 200ms after the card lands, at every size: rating-height on the 20th-place hero, name-height on standard cards, small on compact cards.

4 Share

Tap: the button flashes for 90ms. The 1080×1350 image feeds out of the card top-down in 360ms (clip 100% to 0, y −28 to 0, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)) and goes straight to the system share sheet. Nothing stays in the card.
